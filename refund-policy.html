Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

CEE Beauty Studio aims to provide a clear and fair process for customers who have concerns about online product orders.

1. Product Returns

Unopened products may be considered for return, subject to applicable South African law and confirmation of the return conditions by CEE Beauty Studio.

For hygiene and product-safety reasons, customers should not open, use or tamper with products they intend to return. This does not remove any rights customers may have under applicable law.

2. Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Orders

If an order arrives damaged, appears defective or contains an incorrect item, please contact us as soon as reasonably possible.

Please provide:

  • Your order number or proof of purchase.
  • A description of the issue.
  • Clear photographs of the product and packaging, where relevant.

We will review the issue and advise on the appropriate next steps in accordance with applicable law.

3. How to Request a Return

Contact CEE Beauty Studio before sending a product back. Please wait for return instructions rather than sending an item without prior arrangement.

WhatsApp or telephone: 071 661 8653

4. Refunds

Approved refunds will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the circumstances of the order.

The refund method, processing timeframe and responsibility for return delivery costs must be confirmed with CEE Beauty Studio before this policy is finalised.

5. Salon Services and Deposits

This policy concerns online retail products. Salon appointment deposits are governed separately by the salon's booking policies.

The salon's booking terms state that a 50% non-refundable deposit is required to secure an appointment. Cancellations require at least 48 hours' notice, rescheduling is permitted once with sufficient notice, and no-shows forfeit their deposit.

These terms do not override any rights that may apply under South African law.

6. Contact Us

For return or refund enquiries, contact CEE Beauty Studio on 071 661 8653.
