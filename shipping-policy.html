Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

At CEE Beauty Studio, we aim to process and dispatch your orders as efficiently as possible. This policy explains how online orders are processed and delivered.

1. Delivery Coverage

We offer delivery within South Africa using The Courier Guy. Delivery availability may depend on the delivery address and courier service coverage.

2. Order Processing

Orders are generally processed within 1–2 business days after payment has been confirmed. Business days exclude weekends and public holidays.

Order processing time is separate from the courier's delivery time. Processing may take longer if we need to confirm order details or resolve a payment issue. If a significant delay occurs, we will aim to contact you.

3. Delivery Fees and Delivery Times

Delivery fees and estimated delivery times should be confirmed before an order is completed. The fee and delivery estimate may depend on your delivery address and the courier service.

Once your delivery details have been confirmed, any applicable delivery charge should be communicated before payment.

4. Delivery Address

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address, contact number and any relevant delivery instructions.

Incorrect or incomplete information may cause delays or additional delivery arrangements.

5. Delivery and Receipt

Please ensure that someone is available to receive the parcel at the supplied address. If a delivery cannot be completed, further arrangements may be required with the courier.

6. Delays Outside Our Control

Delivery estimates may be affected by courier operations, weather, public holidays, high delivery volumes or other circumstances outside our control. We will do our best to assist if a delivery is delayed.

7. Damaged, Missing or Incorrect Items

Please contact us as soon as possible if your order arrives damaged, is missing an item, or contains an incorrect product. Include your order details and clear photographs where applicable so we can investigate and advise you on the next steps.

Your rights under applicable consumer protection laws remain unaffected.
